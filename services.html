How It Works

1

Brief & Discovery

We discuss your book's genre, target audience, and visual references. I learn about the mood, key characters, settings, and what makes your story unique. You fill out a short creative brief that guides the entire project.

2

Concept & Sketch

I create 2-3 rough sketches exploring different compositions, lighting directions, and color approaches. You review and select the direction that best captures your book's atmosphere.

3

Revision & Refinement

Based on your feedback, I refine the chosen sketch into a full-color, fully rendered illustration. Up to 3 rounds of revisions are included to ensure every detail is right — from character features to background atmosphere.

4

Final Delivery

You receive print-ready and digital files in all required formats: PDF with bleed and trim marks, high-resolution TIFF/PNG (300 DPI), and web-optimized JPG. Source files available upon request.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a book cover cost?

Pricing depends on complexity, usage rights, and deadline. Typical range for a detailed sci-fi or fantasy cover is $800-2500. Contact me for a custom quote tailored to your project.

What file formats do you deliver?

Print-ready PDF, high-resolution TIFF/PNG (300 DPI), and web-optimized JPG. Source PSD files are available upon request. All files include proper bleed and trim marks.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes. Up to 3 rounds of revisions are included in every project. Additional revision rounds can be arranged if needed.

Can you match an existing series style?

Yes. I regularly create covers that match existing series aesthetics. I analyze the typography, layout, color palette, and illustration style to ensure consistency across all books.

How do I pay?

Payment via direct bank transfer, MoneyGram, or cryptocurrency (USDT, BTC, ETH). A 50% deposit is required to start, with the remainder due before final delivery.
