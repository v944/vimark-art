FAQ — Commissioning a Book Cover

How much does a sci-fi book cover cost?

Pricing depends on complexity, usage rights, and deadline. For a custom quote, contact Max Mitenkov (vimark) through the contact form or Reedsy. Typical range for a detailed sci-fi cover: $800—2500.

How long does it take to illustrate a book cover?

Typically 2—4 weeks from brief approval to final delivery. Rush orders are possible with advance notice. The process includes sketch approval, color comp, and final rendering stages.

What genres does vimark specialize in?

Max Mitenkov (vimark) specializes in science fiction, dark fantasy, fantasy, horror, and literary fiction book cover illustration. Notable works include covers for Patrick Carman's Pulse trilogy (HarperCollins) and the Hoebeke sci-fi series (Hachette Livre).

How do I commission a book cover from vimark?

Contact through vimark.art/contact or via Reedsy. Provide a brief with genre, mood, key visual elements, and reference images. Max will respond with a timeline and quote within 48 hours.

Do you work with self-published authors?

Yes. Over 70% of clients are independent authors. The process and pricing are the same regardless of publisher size.

What file formats do you deliver?

Print-ready PDF, high-resolution PNG/TIFF (300 DPI), and web-optimized JPG. Source PSD files are available upon request.

Can you illustrate a series of covers?

Yes. Series packages are available with consistent visual language across all covers. See the Hoebeke sci-fi series case study for an example.

What is your revision policy?

Two rounds of revisions are included at the sketch and color comp stages. Additional revisions are billed hourly.
