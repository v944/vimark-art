Civilisations

2026 · Renaud Jesionek

Discover the foundations of human civilization through the greatest works of science fiction: art, economy, education, food, health, institutions, languages, leisure, love, media, religion, science, transportation, urban planning and work. How do imaginary worlds organize life, power and culture — and what do they reveal about our own society? Always striving for exploration, Renaud Jesionek, aka Captain Nexus VI, deciphers the blueprints of fictional civilizations in all their forms. Science fiction is the perfect journey.
